- Clean, simple black-and-white document style (no colored branding/headers)
- Uses bullet points (•) for each field
- Format: "Label: ________________" with blank lines for fill-in
- Section headers are bold, underlined, numbered (1. Basic Business Information, 2. Funding Request Details, etc.)
- No colored backgrounds, no fancy styling — just a clean form document

- 3 pages total, standard letter size, generous margins
- No header/footer branding — just the content
